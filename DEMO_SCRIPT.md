# 2–3 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem
“Most reading practice feels like an exercise sheet. StoryQuest turns reading into an adventure where the child has a reason to keep reading.”

## 0:20–0:45 — Home / quest
Show the learner's streak, XP, stars and today's adventure. Start a story.

## 0:45–1:20 — Read to power the world
Read the first story section aloud. Show the Voice Power meter and animated story-world cues responding to reading progress.

Say: “Reading is not just being measured — it is what powers the adventure.”

## 1:20–1:50 — AI reading insight
Finish reading. Show Gemini's structured reading insight: accuracy/fluency, words to revisit, strength and next focus.

Say: “The app uses AI to turn the reading session into a personalized learning signal.”

## 1:50–2:20 — Detective + choice
Complete the Story Detective challenge, then show the two story choices. Pick one and show the consequence that changes the next scene.

Say: “Comprehension becomes part of the game, and the learner's choice changes what happens next.”

## 2:20–2:45 — Word Wizard + rewards
Complete the vocabulary challenge. Show XP, stars, badge progress and the word power-up.

## 2:45–3:00 — Close
“The goal is simple: make children want to keep reading because reading is how they unlock the adventure — while AI helps personalize what they learn next.”
